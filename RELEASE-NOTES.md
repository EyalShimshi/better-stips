## Stips+ 0.18: the bell in sentences, link cards, updates inside the app, and tips

**Notifications (#27)**
- A question's updates are one row that reads as one sentence with the total: "התקבלו 6 תשובות חדשות לשאלה: …". No arrow, no "N updates" line, and the same on your phone.
- No summary line at the top. Older notifications load by themselves as you scroll.
- Flowers are drawn in Stips' own tulip colors, everywhere in the app.
- Tapping a phone notification marks it read, and the bell's number drops right away.
- A notice from Stips' team opens in full, from its phone notification too.

**Chats**
- **Link cards:** a link to a Stips question shows as a card in the message (its photo, title and answers), and a profile link shows the person (photo, badge, credibility, answers and flowers). Tap to open it in the app. A link on its own line hides behind its card, like carets do.
- **Reply in private:** a question's ⋮ menu → *Reply in private* opens a chat with the asker, with the question attached above the box. It's also a choice for swipes, on questions and on other people's answers (Settings → Home, and Questions and answers).

**Updates and sharing**
- **Updates inside the app (#25):** once a day Stips+ asks GitHub for a newer build. Home then shows a card: *Update* downloads it, checks it's exactly the published file, and Android installs it after you confirm. Settings → About can check now, or turn the daily check off.
- **The app's page (#24):** www.shimshii.com/stips-plus, in Hebrew and English, with the newest build to download. Your bio's "I use Stips+" line links there now.
- **Share → Stips+:** share a Stips link from any app (WhatsApp, the browser) to Stips+ and it opens in the app.

**Profiles, settings and more**
- **Bios (#29):** up to 1000 characters, centered, and a last line in tildes (`~your words~`) shows under the bio as your quote, as on Stips.
- **Moderator badges (#31)** always show, and are checked again every day. The setting is gone.
- **Calmer refresh (#30):** after you pull to refresh, rows change in place instead of sliding over each other.
- **Credit (#26):** a small line at the bottom of Settings and on the intro's last page, and a badge on my profile.
- **Tips (#28):** small hints teach the swipes as you meet them (answers, your own answer, chat messages, Home's two swipes, as you set them), plus Live's long press and your notes. Each shows until you use it, three times at most.

**To test**
1. The bell: a busy question reads as one sentence with the total. Scroll far down and older ones keep coming.
2. A phone notification: tap it, then check that the bell's number dropped.
3. Your bio: add a last line like `~ my words ~` (Edit bio). It shows under the bio as a quote.
4. Send yourself (notes to self) a question link and a profile link. You should see their cards.
5. A question by someone else → ⋮ → *Reply in private*. Send it, then look at the card in the chat.
6. Settings → About → *Show the tips again*, then open Home, a question with answers, and a chat.
7. Settings → About → *Check for updates*. Next time a build comes out, Home offers it.
