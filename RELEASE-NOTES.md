## Stips+ 0.19: share to chats, a menu for messages, bigger flowers

**Sharing**
- **Share to chats:** share text from any app (WhatsApp, the browser, notes) and pick **Stips+**. Your notes to self are on top, then your recent chats (more load as you scroll), and the search finds anyone on Stips. Pick up to **five** and send. Five is a fixed limit, so this can't be used to spam.
- Sent to one chat, that chat opens. Sent to several, you're back where you were with "Sent to 3 chats".
- Text only: formatting, pictures and files are left out. Sharing a Stips link no longer opens it in the app. Tapping a Stips link still does, if Stips+ opens Stips links (Android's *Open by default*).

**Chats**
- **Long-press a message** for a menu: *Reply* (like the swipe), *Copy*, *Forward* (to up to five chats) and *Share* (to other apps). Forward and Share leave out the reply carets at the start, since they point into this chat.

**Questions**
- The flower on a flowered answer, and the one in the *Send a flower* button, are now as big as the answerer's photo.
- The "Stips+ creator" badge shows on my profile only, not next to my answers and questions.

**Settings**
- The credit at the end reads "נוצר באהבה על ידי אייל שימשי ❤️", and there's a *Buy me a coffee* button next to the profile button.

**The app's page**
- www.shimshii.com is now my site, with my apps on it, and Stips+'s page at www.shimshii.com/stips-plus looks like the app.

**To test**
1. In another app (say WhatsApp or Chrome), share some text and pick Stips+. Check that notes come first, then pick one chat and send: that chat should open with the message.
2. Share again and pick three. Try a sixth pick: it should say five is the most. Send, and check the "Sent to 3 chats" message.
3. In a chat, long-press a message that starts with carets (a reply) and tap *Forward*. The preview at the top shouldn't have the carets. Try *Share* and *Copy* too.
4. Open a question with a flowered answer: the flower should be as big as the answerer's photo.
5. Open one of your answers: no creator badge next to your name. Your profile still has it.
6. Settings → scroll to the end → *Buy me a coffee*.
7. If you have 0.18 installed, Home should offer this build on its update card. This is the first real test of updates inside the app.
